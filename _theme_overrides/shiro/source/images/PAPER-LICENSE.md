# Paper texture attribution

The active reading texture, `paper-handmade.webp`, is a lossless WebP conversion
of **Handmade Paper**, created by **Le Marquis**. It was originally published by
Subtle Patterns and distributed in transparent form by Transparent Textures.

- Author and texture: https://www.transparenttextures.com/handmade-paper.html
- Downloaded source: https://www.transparenttextures.com/patterns/handmade-paper.png
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (see below)

Its pixels and transparency are unchanged. CSS repeats it at a modest scale,
uses multiply blending in the light theme and inverted screen blending in the
dark theme, and controls its opacity. The raised
fiber detail comes from the texture itself. Attribution is retained beside
the CSS rule.

`paper-fibers.webp`, retained as a source asset, is a lossless WebP conversion of **Paper Fibers**, created by
**Heliodor Jalba**, originally published by Subtle Patterns and distributed in
transparent form by Transparent Textures.

- Author and texture: https://www.transparenttextures.com/paper-fibers.html
- Downloaded source: https://www.transparenttextures.com/patterns/paper-fibers.png
- Original collection: https://www.toptal.com/designers/subtlepatterns/
- Collection license statement: https://github.com/atlemo/SubtlePatterns/blob/gh-pages/README.md
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

Both converted textures remain under CC BY-SA 3.0. Neither relies on a request
to the texture service during normal builds or page loads.

`paper-edge-*.svg` are separate, project-created decorative masks. Their edge
depth stays fixed in CSS pixels, so it does not stretch with long articles.
